Invoice Sales Data

Track cost, revenue, and profit margins directly on customer invoices

Key Features

Invoice-Level Tracking

Cost and profit on every customer invoice and credit note

Partner Analytics

Lifetime profit and average per sale on every customer record

Real-time Updates

Recent invoices update automatically. No manual calculation

Historical Freeze

Invoices older than 7 days are frozen. Product cost changes won't affect history

One-Time Compute

All historical invoices compute on install. After that, only recent ones recalculate

Admin-Only Visibility

All fields require Billing Administrator role. Keep margin data private

How It Works

1

Install and Automatic Computation

On install, the module computes cost and profit for all historical invoices. Takes 30-60 seconds for 10k invoices

2

View Invoice Sales Data

Open any invoice to see Cost and Profit in the Other Info tab. Profit column appears in list views

3

Analyze Customer Performance

See Total Profit and Avg Profit Per Sale in Sales and Purchase tab. Identify your most profitable customers

Partner Sales Analytics

Total Profit

Lifetime net profit from all invoices and credits. True value of each customer relationship

Avg Per Sale

Average profit per invoice. Excludes credit notes to avoid artificially lowering the average

Why exclude credits from average? Credit notes inflate transaction count and have negative values. We count only actual sales for the average but include credits in total profit

Technical Highlights

Stored Computed Fields

Recalculates only when invoice lines, products, or costs change. No unnecessary processing

Product Price Caching

Queries each unique product once and caches in memory. 100x faster than individual database queries

Bulk SQL Updates

Updates 500 invoices at once with raw SQL instead of individual writes. Dramatically reduces installation time
