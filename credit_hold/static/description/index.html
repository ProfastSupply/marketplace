Everything You Need

A complete credit risk workflow - no third-party apps required

Per-Customer Credit Limits

Set a custom monetary limit on any customer record. Open and partially-paid invoices are summed automatically and compared against the limit.

Automatic Hold Detection

When a customer's outstanding balance crosses their credit limit, the Account On Hold flag is set automatically - no manual review required.

Native Approval Gate

Built on Odoo's Studio Approval system. When a held customer's order is confirmed, approvers are notified and must approve before the order proceeds.

Smart Visual Banners

Sales staff see a blue info banner while building an order for a held customer. The banner changes to amber once approval has been requested.

Labeled Approval Activities

Approval activities are automatically labeled "Credit Hold - Approval Required" so approvers can distinguish them at a glance in their activity inbox.

Configurable Approvers

Choose one or more approvers and a notification recipient from Settings. Changes sync instantly to the approval rule - no developer needed.

How It Works

From invoice overdue to order approved in four steps

1

Set a Credit Limit

Open any customer record and enter a credit limit under Sales & Purchase > Credit Management. Leave it at zero for unlimited credit.

2

Run the Balance Check

Your scheduled script sums all invoices with payment state of not paid or partial. If the total exceeds the limit, Account On Hold is ticked and an activity is created for the notification recipient.

3

Sale Orders Enter the Approval Queue

Any quotation for a held customer shows a blue banner. When the salesperson clicks Confirm, the order is sent for approval - the banner turns amber and the approver receives an activity labeled Credit Hold - Approval Required.

4

Approve, Reject, or Wait for the Hold to Lift

Approvers click the avatar icon next to Confirm to approve or reject. Once the customer's balance drops below their limit, the hold lifts automatically and the order can be confirmed normally.

Quick Setup

Navigate to Sales > Configuration > Settings > Credit Hold

Credit Hold Settings
Sales > Configuration > Settings
Credit Hold Approvers
One or more users who can approve or reject blocked sale orders. They receive a Grant Approval activity when any held customer's order is submitted.
Notification Recipient
The user who receives an activity when an account is placed on hold or released. Typically your credit controller or AR manager.
Credit Limit (per customer)
Set individually on each customer record under Sales & Purchase > Credit Management. Supports any currency tied to the customer's pricelist.
